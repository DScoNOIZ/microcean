Mathematical Barriers (Unavoidable)

These limits are not implementation defects — they are theorems. No engineering can bypass them; Microcean acknowledges them and designs around them.

BarrierResultStatusConsequence for the concept
Kolmogorov complexity K(x)uncomputable (Solomonoff 1964; Li & Vitányi 2008)Impossible"Zero excess" is a heuristic (MDL), not a mathematical optimum. Absolute assembly minimality is unattainable.
No Free Lunch (Wolpert & Macready 1997)no universal optimizer for all tasksImpossibleMicrocean's strength is on the class of tasks with structure (low epistasis, clean interfaces).
Amdahl's Law (Amdahl 1967)speedup bounded by sequential fractionProvenParallelism of assembly limited by coordination/verification (E8: p=0.99 → S(1024)=91.2).
Halting Problem / Rice's Theoremabsolute error-freeness unattainableImpossibleAttainable are tests on finite sets and verification of bounded properties (CEGIS soundness).
Schema theorem ≠ GA convergence guaranteeexpectation of schema growth ≠ dynamics equationCaveatEvolutionary search may stall; elitism and restarts mitigate but do not eliminate.

What Microcean Does NOT Promise

  • A universal assembler for "any program" — only "a wide class of tasks with structure" (No Free Lunch).
  • Absolute code minimality — only heuristic shrinkage (K(x) uncomputable).
  • Frictionless parallelism — Amdahl's law sets the ceiling (E8: 91.2× at 1024 cores).
  • Guaranteed evolutionary convergence — schema theorem gives expectation, not determinism.
  • Sybil immunity without economic stakes — theoretical lower bound (Douceur 2002).
  • Legal invulnerability — defensive prior art protects what is disclosed, but patents on unrelated improvements remain possible.
  • Immunity to "fragment garbage" without economic incentives — stakes and MDL pressure required.
