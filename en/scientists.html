MICROCEAN
One ocean from a billion drops

The formal model of the assembly task

Definition (the fragment space). ℱ = {g₁, …, gN_F} — a finite set of micro-code fragments. Each fragment g = (code(g), meta(g)), where code(g) is executable source code over an alphabet Σ; meta(g) is the interface (signature), pre-/post-conditions, a hash, reputation, semantic tags, as well as the measured performance profile (benchmarks of time, memory and traffic — part of the fragment's passport).

Definition (assembly). An assembly is a subset S ⊆ ℱ together with a composition operation ∘ that glues the fragments into a program p ∈ 𝒫.

Problem statement. Given a task T with the specification Spec(T). It is required to find:

argminS ⊆ ℱ  Φ(S) = α·Err(S, Spec(T)) + β·cost(p(S)) + γ·incomp(S)

where Err is the mismatch to the specification (failed tests), cost is the complete resource footprint of the program: size, running time, memory, network traffic, energy (an implementation of the principles of "zero excess" and resource responsibility), incomp — conflicts of interfaces; α ≫ β, γ by default. Constraints: typability of the assembly, pairwise compatibility of interfaces, a budget cost(p) ≤ B. The presence of cost in the objective function means that evolutionary selection cuts off wasteful combinations automatically: optimization is built into the mechanism of selection, rather than being a separate "when it pinches" stage.

A remark on complexity. The search space is 2N_F subsets. At N_F ~ 10⁶ an exact solution by enumeration is impossible. Granularity: plankton (micro-fragments) → schools of fish (functions/modules) → islands (packages/apps) → ecosystems (stacks) — NO rigid gradation. Torrent-for-code at the micro-level: P2P, take exactly what you need, no excess traffic.

The evolutionary engine

Theoretical anchors

  • Holland's schema theorem [proven]. Short, low-order schemas with above-average fitness grow exponentially in number: E[m(H,t+1)] ≥ m(H,t)·(f̂(H,t)/f̄(t))·[1 − p_c·d(H)/(L−1) − o(H)·p_m]. Caveat: the inequality is about an expectation, not an equation of dynamics.
  • The Price equation [proven]. w̄·Δz̄ = Cov(wᵢ, zᵢ) + E(wᵢ·Δzᵢ) — the fundamental decomposition of evolutionary dynamics.
  • Building Block Hypothesis [hypothesis] (Goldberg, 1989). A working assumption of low epistasis between well-isolated fragments; counterexamples are known (deceptive functions).
  • Sizing per Goldberg–Deb–Clark [proven in the accepted noise model]. N_s = 2cκσ²_M/d², where c is the square of the one-sided normal deviate: for a tolerable decision error α = 0.05 we have z = 1.645, c = z² ≈ 2.71. The empirical success threshold N* = 50–100 against the theoretical estimate 173 (at σ_M/d = 4): the theory leaves a margin, the empirics are more modest — the discrepancy is explainable by elitism on true fitness.

Numerical results (seed = 42, reproducible)

E2 — GA convergence (L=20, M=10): success 30/30 at N ∈ {50, 100, 200}; the first hit of the optimum in generation 38.7 / 25.3 / 20.6 (mean). The budget ≈ N·G = 2·10⁴ evaluations against 10¹⁰¹ of enumeration — the GA examines a fraction ≈10⁻⁹⁷ of the space and does not work by enumeration.

E3 — epistasis: at a fraction of pairwise interactions λ ≤ 0.3 the correctness is 1.0; at λ = 0.7 it falls to 0.325 (0/30 reach the optimum). This justifies the requirement of "clean interfaces" between fragments.

E4 — sizing vs empirics: at σ_M/d = 4 the theoretical GDC N_s = 173 (κ=2, c=z²≈2.71), the empirical threshold N* = 50–100; the theory is conservative by a factor of 1.7–3.5 (elitism on true fitness).

The swarm of micro-networks: ensemble bounds

The bias–variance decomposition [Geman et al., 1992]: E[(y−f̂(x))²] = Bias² + Var + σ². For an ensemble of M models with pairwise error correlation ρ: Var → ρσ² as M→∞ — the diversity of the experts (low ρ) plays the key role. Specialization via sparse MoE [Shazeer et al., 2017, arXiv:1701.06538] — a "sparse mixture of experts". Agents as the new assembly layer: they assemble the program from distributed fragments and modules; current human tools give neither a unified database, nor a convenient torrent-like interface, nor homogeneous practices — which is why this environment is morally obsolete for machine labor.

The bound [plausible]: there is no theorem of universal superiority of the swarm (NFL); the gain is achieved in latency, fault tolerance and transfer-efficiency; the comparison "swarm vs one big model" is an empirical question.

Synthesis from a specification: CEGIS

The SyGuS framework [Alur et al., 2013]: find f ∈ ℋ such that ∃f ∀x ∈ 𝒟: φ(x, f(x)). The "synthesizer–verifier" loop: soundness is proven; termination at a finite grammar is proven; completeness in the general case is unattainable.

E9 (toy): max(x,y) synthesized as ite((x<y), y, x) in 1 CEGIS iteration (820 candidates, 0.05 s); post-verification on a grid of 1600 points — 0 discrepancies. The expressiveness boundary: without a comparison operator the task is inexpressible in the grammar {+, *, ite} — an honest boundary of the method.

The P2P layer: proven bounds

  • Kademlia [proven], O(log n). E5 (n=10⁹, k=20, 1000 lookups): mean 5.42 ± 0.83 hops, maximum 8 (the upper bound ceil log₂ n = 30).
  • Gossip push-pull [proven], O(log n) [Karp et al., FOCS 2000]. E6: n=10⁵ → 11.37 ± 0.48 rounds at log₃ n = 10.48 (ratio 1.08 and shrinking as n grows).
  • CRDT [proven] [Shapiro et al., 2011]. A join-semilattice = a commutative idempotent monoid → eventual consistency. E7: 10000/10000 checks of commutativity/associativity/idempotence for GCounter, GSet, ORSet; 1000/1000 replica-convergence scenarios.
  • Amdahl [proven]. E8: p=0.99 → S(1024)=91.2, the limit 100; the sequential part (coordination, verification) sets the ceiling of the speedup of distributed assembly.

"Zero excess": MDL and Kolmogorov complexity

The MDL principle [Rissanen, 1978; Grünwald, 2007]: L(D) = min_H [L(H) + L(D|H)] — a formalization of Occam's razor. The Kolmogorov complexity K(x) is uncomputable; any practical minimization is an approximation.

E10 (a): duplicate dead fragments have almost no effect on the zlib size (159 → 265 with k: 0 → 200); unique dead fragments grow both the raw and the zlib metric. (b): a GA with an MDL penalty shortens the assembly by a factor of 2.5 (150.9 → 60.9) while preserving correctness 1.0. (c): the Pearson correlation of the zlib length with correctness ≈ −0.04 — compression captures the structure of the content, not the "neededness"; the boundary of the MDL heuristic is fixed.

Additional caveats: the schema theorem ≠ a guarantee of GA convergence; hashgraph aBFT — a protocol property under its assumptions; the Tangle — probabilistic consensus; the quantum perspectives of the concept lie outside the current mathematical base and are marked as a direction. Harmony: atoms, not bricks; everything flows like currents; no bottlenecks, rough seams, brittle stitches. This is not a fragmentary, heterogeneous, poorly connected system — but one environment forming a harmonious whole: everything interconnected, everything flowing, no narrow passages, bottlenecks, absurd pile-ups, brittle stitches.
