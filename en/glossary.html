Microcean Glossary

Key terms of the concept in alphabetical order. Anglicisms kept in English where standard in the profession.

Agent
An autonomous software executor solving a narrow task (fragment assessment, conflict finding, assembly). In Microcean, assembly is performed by a swarm of narrow agents, not one universal AI.
Amdahl's Law
Parallelism bounded by the sequential coordination fraction. E8: at p=0.99 the speedup ceiling on 1024 cores = 91.2× (limit 100×).
Anchor References (ref, multi_ref, transform)
References to fragments inside an assembly. Allow an agent to point to a ready piece instead of regenerating it.
Archipelago
Metaphor for today's software landscape: scattered islands (repositories, packages, IDEs, CI/CD) weakly connected by bridges. Microcean proposes the Ocean — continuous water instead of an archipelago.
Assembly
A subset of fragments S ⊆ ℱ with a composition operation ∘ gluing them into a program p. Goal: argmin Φ(S) = α·Err + β·cost + γ·incomp.
Bridge
Selective exchange between a lagoon and the open Ocean under the lagoon owner's rules: something is imported, something never leaves the circuit.
Cambrian Explosion
Metaphor: as in the ocean of life — sudden diversity of forms. In the Code Ocean — when fragments begin to combine freely, an explosive number of new programs is born.
CEGIS / Counterexample-Guided Inductive Synthesis
SyGuS framework (Alur et al. 2013). Synthesizer–verifier loop: soundness proven; termination at finite grammar proven; completeness unattainable in general.
Client
A program connecting to the Ocean protocol. Multiple clients welcome; no vendor lock-in, no API keys at the protocol level.
Compromise
Attack on a fragment. In the Ocean: no monoculture — compromise of one fragment does not destroy the system; word of the threat spreads via currents.
Coordination
The sequential part of assembly setting the parallelism ceiling via Amdahl's law. Top-level swarm management.
CRDT / Conflict-free Replicated Data Type
Join-semilattice = commutative idempotent monoid → eventual consistency. Used for fragment registry state and queue-of-rights (ORSet for multi-author). E7: 10000/10000 checks passed.
Currents
Movement of fragments between participants. P2P layer on Kademlia (O(log n) lookup) + Gossip push-pull (O(log n) update propagation).
Defensive Prior Art
Publication of the concept (2026-08-11/10-22) creates prior art: no one can patent what is disclosed. CC0 1.0 Universal + RF Civil Code Art.1265/1267 (inalienable authorship).
Ecosystems
Metaphor for full stacks: constellations of islands (packages/apps) living in symbiosis. Large life form in the Ocean.
Epistasis
Interaction between fragments. λ ≤ 0.3 → correctness 1.0; λ = 0.7 → 0.325 (E3). "Clean interfaces" requirement is the architectural answer.
Evolution / Evolutionary Selection
Assembly variants compete; best combinations preserved and developed — by evolutionary biology and genetic algorithm principles. Elitism + restarts mitigate lack of convergence guarantee.
Fragment (Micro-fragment)
The basic unit of code — a small fragment (function, algorithm) with interface, pre/post-conditions, hash, reputation, semantic tags, measured performance profile. Not a large package.
Garbage Flood (Fragment Garbage)
Risk: Ocean fills with millions of low-quality/duplicate/malicious fragments; search slows, reputation dilutes. Mitigation: economic stake on publication, MDL auto-eviction, lagoon filters, citation graph weight.
GDC Sizing (Goldberg–Deb–Clark)
N_s = 2cκσ²_M/d². At σ_M/d=4: theoretical 173.19 (κ=2, c=z²≈2.71); empirical N* = 50–100 (factor 1.7–3.5). Elitism on true fitness explains gap.
Gossip Protocol
Epidemic dissemination; eventual consistency. Fragment update propagation; reputation gossip. Karp et al. 2000; E6: n=10⁵ → 11.37 ± 0.48 rounds.
Granularity / No Rigid Gradation
Plankton (micro-fragments) → schools of fish (functions/modules) → islands (packages/apps) → ecosystems (stacks). No sharp boundaries — take exactly what you need: drops, a school, an island.
Halting Problem / Rice's Theorem
Absolute error-freeness unattainable. Attainable: tests on finite sets and verification of bounded properties (CEGIS soundness).
Kademlia DHT
XOR-metric distributed hash table; O(log n) lookup. Fragment discovery in Microcean's P2P layer. E5: n=10⁹ → mean 5.42 ± 0.83 hops, max 8.
Kolmogorov Complexity K(x)
Minimal program length producing x. Uncomputable (Solomonoff 1964; Li & Vitányi 2008). "Zero excess" is an MDL heuristic, not a mathematical optimum.
Lagoon
Closed circuit of an organization/state: its own fragments, its own history, zero leaks. Self-sufficient but poorer without external inflow. Selective exchange (bridge) and ecosystem fork possible.
Legal Standing
CC0 1.0 Universal; inalienable authorship (RF Civil Code Art.1265/1267); defensive prior art; author not operator/not beneficiary/not founder; triple timestamping (DOI/OTS/notary) in preparation.
Library/Package
"Large inhabitants" of the Ocean — whales and coral reefs. Mature forms refined by evolution; used whole, copied, tamed. But the foundation of all life is the plankton of micro-fragments.
Lookup / Kademlia Probe
Finding a fragment in a network of 10⁹ nodes: mean 5.42 ± 0.83 hops (E5), max 8. Upper bound ceil log₂ n = 30.
MDL / Minimum Description Length
Rissanen 1978: L(D) = min_H [L(H) + L(D|H)] — formalization of Occam's razor. In Microcean: size penalty in objective; E10: assembly 2.5× shorter at correctness 1.0.
No Free Lunch (Wolpert & Macready 1997)
No universal optimizer for all tasks. Microcean's strength is on the class of tasks with structure (low epistasis, clean interfaces).
Objective Function Φ
Φ(S) = α·Err(S, Spec) + β·cost(p(S)) + γ·incomp(S). α ≫ β, γ. cost = complete resource footprint: size, time, memory, traffic, energy.
Ocean
Single environment where all planetary software lives: code, history, practices. Exchange, work, storage, passing on — neighboring areas of one sea. Not an archipelago.
Plankton
Metaphor for micro-fragments: tiniest, most numerous, foundation of the Ocean's entire food chain. From a billion drops — one water.
Queue-of-Rights
Immutable rights history on fragment in CRDT (ORSet for multi-author). Operational transformation on conflicts. Queue-of-rights = law in code.
Reputation
History of passed executable tests + resource profile. Fragment reputation accrues on citation graph; author reputation through fragment quality.
Schema Theorem (Holland) ≠ GA Convergence Guarantee
Expectation of schema growth ≠ dynamics equation. Evolutionary search may stall; elitism and restarts mitigate but do not eliminate.
Schools of Fish
Metaphor for functions/modules: groups of fragments working in concert. More plankton, fewer islands.
Stake
Economic bond on fragment correctness at publication. Sybil and garbage-flood protection. Slashed on detected maliciousness.
Sybil Attack / Sybil Resistance
Douceur 2002: impossible without external identity. Microcean: economic stake at ocean entry + reputation on fragment citation graph, not on nodes.
Swarm
Multitude of narrow specialized executors (neural and algorithmic). Each solves one task. High-class models requested pointwise — where semantic understanding is needed.
Throughput
Ocean throughput bounded by Amdahl's law: sequential coordination/verification sets parallelism ceiling.
Transparency
All fragments, their history, reputation, tests — open and verifiable. No black boxes.
Two-Date Chain
Internal fixation 2026-08-11 in concept's working materials + public disclosure 2026-10-22 on GitHub. Commit history = verifiable date proof.
Verifier Races
Risk: different verifiers give contradictory verdicts on same fragment; consensus fails. Mitigation: reference test suite, PBFT on results, appeal via re-verification.
Version History / Sedimentary Layers
Change history in the Ocean does not get lost — it settles on the seabed as geological strata. Any version of any fragment available for audit and reuse.
Waste / Zero Excess
Principle: don't drag along what you don't use. Basic unit of reuse = fragment of exactly needed size. Efficiency tests equal to correctness tests.
Working Materials / Drafts
Internal concept materials where 2026-08-11 is recorded. Not to be confused with public disclosure 2026-10-22.
Zero Excess (Ноль лишнего)
Principle: don't carry what you don't use. Base unit of reuse = fragment of exactly needed size. Efficiency tests equal to correctness tests.
